Why This Matters

COmPOSER treats passives as layout-aware choices, not after-layout substitutions. Instead, geometry selection and primitive realization happen early enough that placement and routing can make physically meaningful choices.

Decap Power Pins

The connected-PDN generator reads FIXED_PRIMITIVES/auto_gen_decap_lna_0_1.gds. For LNA_1, PLUS connects to net3; MINUS, BULK1, and BULK2 connect to gnd. Generated instances are added only to PDN-specific placement and LEF files.
